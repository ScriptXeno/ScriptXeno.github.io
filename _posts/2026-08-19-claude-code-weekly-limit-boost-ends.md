## Claude Code's weekly limit boost didn't end on August 19, it got extended again

If someone told you Claude Code's +50% weekly usage boost expires today, August 19, 2026, that was true right up until it wasn't. Checked live today: Anthropic pushed the deadline again, this time to August 31, 2026, and added a line suggesting the boost might stick around permanently. Nothing changes for you today. Something might change on the 31st. Or it might not, again.

### Quick answer

No, the boost is not gone. As of today, August 19, 2026, Anthropic has extended the +50% weekly usage increase for Claude Code a fourth time, now running through August 31, 2026, for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans. Anthropic also said it hopes to make the increase permanent, while flagging that capacity could get tight in the meantime. You don't need to do anything: the boost is still automatic, and your weekly ceiling is still 50 percent above your plan's normal baseline right now.

### The pattern so far

This promotion has now moved four times, and it's worth laying out the actual sequence, because most of what's floating around the web right now is a stale snapshot from whichever deadline was current when it was written.

- May 6, 2026: Anthropic permanently doubles Claude Code's 5-hour rate limits for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise, and removes peak-hour throttling for Pro and Max. This is a separate, permanent change, tied to a new compute deal with SpaceX's Colossus 1 data center.
- May 13, 2026: A different, temporary change starts: a +50% increase to weekly usage limits, originally set to run through July 13.
- Mid-July: extended to July 19.
- July 18: extended again, this time to August 19, the date this post's headline was originally built around.
- Sometime after: extended a fourth time, to August 31, with a note that Anthropic is considering making it permanent.

Anthropic's own posts on the matter, from its ClaudeDevs account, read almost identically each time. The original: "Claude Code weekly limits are increasing 50%, now through July 13. Live now for all Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise users." The August 19 extension: "We're also keeping Claude Code weekly limits 50% higher, now through August 19, for all Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise users." And the latest one, extending to August 31: "We're extending the 50% increase to weekly Claude Code limits through August 31. We hope to make this a permanent change to our plans, but strong demand for our models means that capacity may be tight over the coming weeks. We'll keep you posted as things develop."

That last sentence is the actual news here. Three prior extensions were framed as simple deadline pushes. This one is the first time Anthropic has said, out loud, that it wants the increase to be permanent rather than promotional.

### Why "wants to" is not "will"

Read that quote again and notice what it isn't. It isn't "the increase is now permanent." It's a stated hope, immediately qualified by a warning about tight capacity. Anthropic has been buying and building compute aggressively all year, the SpaceX Colossus deal in May being one example, alongside existing arrangements with Amazon, Google, and Microsoft. More GPUs online generally means more room to hand out generous limits without the accounting getting painful. But "strong demand" cuts the other way: if enough people are hammering Claude Code at once, the calculus that let Anthropic offer 50% more room in May might not hold in September.

Practically, that means treating August 31 the way you should have treated July 13, July 19, and August 19: as the next real date to watch, not a fake deadline that always gets pushed. It might get extended a fifth time. It might quietly become the new baseline with no announcement at all. Or the 50% could actually disappear this time. Anthropic's track record over the last several months points toward another extension being more likely than a hard cutoff, but that's a pattern, not a promise.

### What's permanent regardless of what happens August 31

Two changes from May 6 are unrelated to this promotion and aren't going anywhere: the doubled 5-hour session limit, and the removal of peak-hour throttling for Pro and Max. Those stick whether the weekly boost survives, expires, or gets extended a tenth time. If your main pain point has been getting cut off mid-session rather than hitting a weekly wall, that part of the picture hasn't changed since May and isn't at risk here.

### What actually changes for you right now

Today, nothing. If you're on Pro, Max, Team, or a seat-based Enterprise seat, your weekly limit is still 50% above baseline, applied automatically across the CLI, IDE extensions, desktop app, and web, no toggle to flip and no form to fill out. Free plans and consumption-based Enterprise seats were never part of this to begin with.

What's genuinely annoying is that Anthropic still won't publish the actual numbers behind any of this. There's no public figure for how many hours or tokens a Pro or Max week actually contains, boosted or not, only the plan multipliers (Pro is 1x, Max comes in 5x and 20x tiers). If you want your real, current allocation, check Settings, then Usage on claude.ai, or run the usage command in the CLI. That shows your specific reset time and how much of your window you've burned through, which is more useful than any number in this post or any other.

### If August 31 doesn't go your way

Assume, for a second, that the boost actually lapses next time and your weekly ceiling drops back to its unboosted baseline. Going from 150% of baseline down to 100% is a full third less headroom in the boosted week, not a small trim. If you're someone who runs Claude Code most of the day, that's the difference between finishing the week comfortably and hitting a wall on a Thursday.

### Frequently asked questions

**Did Claude Code's weekly limit boost end on August 19, 2026?**
No. As of today, Anthropic extended it a fourth time, through August 31, 2026. If you're reading this after August 31, check Anthropic's own announcements or your account's usage page, since that date is now the one that matters.

**Do I need to do anything to keep the higher limit?**
No. It applies automatically to eligible plans, no signup, toggle, or code required.

**Which plans get the +50% weekly boost?**
Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans. Free plans and consumption-based Enterprise seats are not included.

**Is this the same as the doubled 5-hour limits from May?**
No, and this is where a lot of the confusion comes from. The 5-hour session limit doubling and the removal of peak-hour throttling, both from May 6, are permanent and separate from this weekly promotion. The weekly +50% boost is the part that keeps getting extended in short increments.

**Will Anthropic actually make the 50% increase permanent?**
Unknown. Anthropic said it hopes to, but paired that with a warning about capacity being tight, which reads like a hedge rather than a commitment. Treat it as a possibility, not a plan.

**What happens if the boost does eventually lapse for good?**
Your weekly ceiling drops back to your plan's standard, unboosted amount, roughly a third less than the boosted week, with no change to billing or your plan otherwise. If that timing is bad for you, the Opencode Zen setup linked above is a working alternative that doesn't depend on Anthropic's promotional calendar at all.
